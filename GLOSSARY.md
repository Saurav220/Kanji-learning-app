# KanjiForge

An offline Japanese kanji study app with flashcards, multiple-choice quizzes, writing practice, and curated study sequences.

## Language

**Multiple-Choice Quiz**:
A study session where each kanji is tested against four candidate answers.
_Avoid_: Quick quiz

**Quiz Focus**:
The aspect of a kanji tested during a Multiple-Choice Quiz — Meanings, Readings, Vocabulary, Sentences, or Mixed questions.
_Avoid_: Quiz prompt
